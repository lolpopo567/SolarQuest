Privacy policy

Solar Quest (web, Android and desktop apps) · last updated 8 October 2026 (on-device version)

Solar Quest is an educational game about choosing sites for solar farms in Thailand. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking, and it runs entirely on your device: there is no game server.

What the game stores

Only on your own device (the browser's or app's local storage): the name you type, your chosen language, and your attempts and scores. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. The "best attempts" table shows only this device's results.

Services the game loads

When online, map tiles may come from MapTiler or OpenStreetMap and street photos from Mapillary. Like any website, they receive your IP address and browser details when your device downloads an image from them. The game sends them nothing else. See the MapTiler, OpenStreetMap and Mapillary privacy policies.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Data & credits on the desktop and choose Erase my name and progress on this device, or uninstall the app / clear the site's data.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information and is suitable for all ages.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact address shown on the game's Google Play page.
