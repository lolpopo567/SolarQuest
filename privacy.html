Privacy policy

Solar Quest (web, Android and desktop apps) · last updated 8 October 2026

Solar Quest is an educational game about choosing sites for solar farms in Thailand. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

What the game stores

DataWhereWhy
A random player IDYour device, and the game serverTo keep your progress and scores. It is generated on your device and is not linked to you.
The name you typeYour device, and the game serverShown in the game and on the leaderboard. Use a nickname if you prefer.
Your attempts and scoresThe game serverProgress, grades and the leaderboard.

The offline desktop app keeps all of this on your computer only.

Services the game loads

Map tiles come from MapTiler and street photos from Mapillary. Like any website, they receive your IP address and browser details when your device downloads an image from them. The game sends them nothing else. See the MapTiler and Mapillary privacy policies.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Your name and best scores appear on the in-game leaderboard for other players to see.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Data & credits on the desktop and choose Delete my name and scores from the server. This removes your name, attempts and leaderboard entries at once. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data removes what is on your device. Server backups are overwritten within 14 days.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information and is suitable for all ages.

Contact

Questions: use the developer contact address shown on the game's Google Play page.
